Case Frameworks Guide

This is a mock reading module page. It demonstrates structured, well-targeted text content for a student audience.

Framework principles

  • MECE: buckets should not overlap; together they cover the problem.
  • Fit the problem: don’t force a generic template onto every prompt.
  • Actionable: each bucket should lead to clear analyses and data requests.

Example buckets (mock)

  • Profitability: revenue (price × volume) and costs (fixed/variable).
  • Market sizing: customers × frequency × spend.
  • Growth strategy: new customers, new products, new geographies, partnerships.

Quick checkpoint

Pick a recent firm prompt and draft a 4-bucket structure. Then add 2 data questions per bucket.
